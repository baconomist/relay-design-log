# Cheapest propane hot tub

16 Oct 2026

The cheapest complete propane hot tub found is the **Joolca HOTTUB Kit, C$1,123** on sale (C$1,248 regular). It is a 2-person inflatable sold with the HOTTAP V2 propane water heater, a 12 V pump and the hoses to recirculate the tub through the heater. No hard-shell hot tub sold with propane heating was found. Hard-shell tubs are electric, and gas heat is a separate pool and spa heater that costs more than a cheap tub does.

## Not priced

- **DIY stock tank + portable propane water heater** (for example a Camplux 41,000 BTU unit, [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/propane-hot-tub-heater/s?k=propane+hot+tub+heater)). This is probably cheaper than Joolca, but no complete kit with a price was found.
- **Nomad Tub**, a collapsible tub with a propane heating coil, was listed at US$449 in an [article about six years old](https://odditymall.com/collapsible-camping-hot-tub/). Weak, and may not be sold any more.

## Gaps

- Whether any propane tub is rated for use below freezing.
- Joolca's Canadian tub-only page says an Off-Grid Plumbing kit is needed for recirculation. The kit above appears to cover it with the 12 V pump and hoses, but confirm with Joolca.
