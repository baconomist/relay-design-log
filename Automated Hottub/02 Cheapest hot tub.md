# Cheapest hot tub of any kind

16 Oct 2026

The cheapest hot tubs are inflatables with a built-in electric heater, starting at about **US$298 to US$398** (Avenli London, 2 to 3 people) in the US and about **C$470** (Naipo, 4 people) at Walmart Canada. The cheapest hard-shell tub found is about **US$2,499**. The catch for us: cheap inflatables are not rated for a Waterloo winter.

## Cheap inflatables and Canadian winter

| Rule | Source | Strength |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Intex: do not leave the spa outside with water below 4°C. Operating range is 4°C to 40°C | [Intex FAQ](https://www.intexdevelopment.com/support/faqlist?catkey=306&lst_lang=2) | Strong, maker |
| Bestway, models without Freeze Shield: not recommended below 10°C outdoors. Another Bestway FAQ page says it cannot run below 4°C | [Bestway spa maintenance FAQ](https://bestwayusa.com/pages/faq-categories/spa-maintenance), [Bestway spas FAQ](https://bestwayusa.com/pages/faq-categories/spas) | Strong, maker, but the two pages conflict |
| Bestway Freeze Shield models hold the water at 6°C to 10°C to stop it freezing | [Bestway blog](https://bestwayusa.com/blog/4-tips-for-winterizing-your-inflatable-hot-tub.html) | Strong, maker |
| One Bestway-type manual: to reach 40°C, outdoor temperature must be at least -5°C. Do not run below -10°C | [Manuals+](https://manuals.plus/bestway/inflatable-spa-manual) | Medium |

Waterloo's January daily mean is **-6.3°C** and February's is **-5.9°C** ([Region of Waterloo airport, 1991 to 2020 normals, Environment Canada, via Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kitchener,_Ontario)). So the cheapest tubs on the market are, by their makers' own rules, summer and shoulder-season products here. That looks like a gap a cheap winter-capable tub could fill, but no one has told us they want it yet.
